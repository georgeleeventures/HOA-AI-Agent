How to Ask a Question

You can ask HouseKeep questions in two ways:

  1. By email: Send your question to your HOA's HouseKeep email address. You'll get a reply within 1-3 minutes.
  2. On the web: Go to the Chat page and type your question. You'll see the answer right away.
No special formatting is needed. Just ask your question in plain English, the same way you'd ask a neighbor.

What You Can Ask About

  • Rules & policies — pet policies, satellite dishes, paint colors, quiet hours, rental rules, modification guidelines
  • Schedules & dates — trash pickup, board meetings, inspections, important deadlines
  • Payments — HOA dues amount, how to set up autopay, payment due dates, what fees cover
  • Maintenance — how to report issues, who to contact, what's covered by HOA fees
  • Ownership info — ownership percentages, shared expense details, voting rights

Example questions:

"Can I install a mini-split AC on my balcony?"
"When is trash pickup day?"
"What is the pet policy?"
"How do I set up autopay for HOA dues?"

Tips for Getting Good Answers

  • Be specific — "Can I paint my front door red?" is better than "What are the rules?"
  • One question per message works best for the most accurate answers
  • Check the sources — HouseKeep shows which documents it used to find the answer
  • It will say "I don't know" rather than make something up

What HouseKeep Cannot Do

HouseKeep is a reference tool. It answers questions but cannot take action on your behalf.
  • Cannot make changes, approve requests, or take actions
  • Cannot give legal advice (but will show exact governing document text)
  • Will not respond when CC'd unless directly asked a question
  • Cannot access personal email — only the HOA's shared account

  • Expense anomalies and trends
"What is our current reserve fund balance?"
"Are we on track with the annual budget?"

Maintenance & Operations

  • Review full maintenance history by unit or vendor
  • Check inspection dates and warranty status
  • Find outstanding requests and deferred maintenance
  • Look up contractor records and past work
"List all outstanding maintenance requests"
"When was the last roof inspection?"

Using HouseKeep in Email Threads

CC HouseKeep on any email thread and it will silently track the conversation. It won't respond or interrupt unless you directly ask it a question.

Later, you can ask: "What was discussed about the roof repair?" and it will recall the thread.

Forward documents to HouseKeep to have them automatically categorized and filed. You'll get a confirmation like: "Filed as Insurance Policy Renewal, expires December 2025."

Legal & Insurance

  • Look up attorney statements and pending legal matters
  • Find insurance policy numbers, expiration dates, and coverage details
  • Check D&O insurance and claims history
  • Review violations and compliance status

Chat

The Chat page lets you have a real-time conversation with HouseKeep. It works just like the email experience but with instant responses.

  • Type your question and press Enter or click Send
  • Click any suggested question to ask it immediately
  • Each answer shows confidence level and source documents
  • Use the thumbs up/down buttons to rate answer quality

Documents

The Documents page lets you browse all HOA documents organized by category:

  • Governing — CC&Rs, bylaws, rules and regulations
  • Financial — budgets, reserve studies, invoices
  • Meeting — board meeting minutes and agendas
  • Maintenance — inspection reports, work orders
  • Insurance — policies, claims, certificates
  • Vendor — contracts, proposals, invoices

Use the search bar to find specific documents by keyword.

Maintenance

The Maintenance page shows a visual timeline of all maintenance work, repairs, and inspections. Each entry includes:

  • Description of the work
  • Unit number and issue type
  • Status (reported, in progress, completed)
  • Contractor name and cost (when available)

Is HouseKeep free?

Yes. HouseKeep is completely free for all HOA residents and administrators. No subscription fees, no per-user charges.

Do I need to download an app?

No. HouseKeep works entirely through email. The web dashboard is optional and accessed through your browser — no download required.

How quickly does it respond?

Most questions are answered within 1-3 minutes by email. Web chat responses are near-instant. Response time may be slightly longer during initial setup.

What if it gives a wrong answer?

HouseKeep always cites source documents so you can verify. If something is wrong, administrators can flag it in the audit log and provide corrections. HouseKeep will say "I don't know" rather than guess.

Can it access my personal email?

No. HouseKeep only accesses the HOA's shared Gmail account. It never reads, sends, or modifies personal email.

Who can see my questions?

Only HOA administrators can view the audit log of questions and answers. Your questions are not visible to other residents.

What file types does it understand?

PDFs, Word documents, Excel spreadsheets, images (JPG, PNG), scanned documents, and photos of printed pages. Video is not supported.

Is there a question limit?

No practical limit. A rate limit of 20 emails per hour prevents abuse, but normal usage will never hit it.
