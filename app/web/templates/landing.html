Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know to get started.

Is HouseKeep really free?
Yes. HouseKeep is completely free for all HOA residents and administrators. No subscription fees, no per-user charges.
Do I need to download an app?
No. HouseKeep works entirely through email. There's also an optional web dashboard you can access by signing in with Google—no download required.
How quickly does it respond?
Most questions are answered within one to three minutes. Response time may be slightly longer during initial setup while your HOA's documents are being processed.
What if it gives a wrong answer?
HouseKeep always cites the source documents behind its answers so you can verify. If something seems off, administrators can flag it in the audit log. HouseKeep will say "I don't know" rather than guess.
Can it access my personal email?
No. HouseKeep only accesses the HOA's shared Gmail account. It never reads, sends, or modifies personal email.
Who can see my questions?
Only HOA administrators can view the audit log of questions and answers. Your questions are not visible to other residents.
Is there a limit to how many questions I can ask?
There's no hard limit. A rate limit of 20 emails per hour is in place to prevent abuse, but normal usage will never hit it.
What file types does it understand?
HouseKeep handles PDFs, Word documents, Excel spreadsheets, images (JPG, PNG), scanned documents, and even photos of printed pages. Video is not supported.
