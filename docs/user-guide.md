# HouseKeep AI — User Guide

## Welcome to HouseKeep AI

HouseKeep AI is your HOA's digital assistant. It reads and organizes all of your HOA's emails and documents — rules, meeting notes, financials, maintenance records — so you can get answers instantly, just by sending an email.

No app to download. No account to create. No training needed. If you can send an email, you can use HouseKeep.

---

## For All Residents

### How to Ask a Question

1. Open your email app (Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail, your phone's email — whatever you use)
2. Start a new email to your HOA's HouseKeep address (your HOA admin will share this with you)
3. Write your question in plain English, just like you'd ask a neighbor
4. Hit send
5. You'll get a response within a few minutes

That's it. No special formatting. No commands. Just write normally.

### What You Can Ask About

**Rules and policies**
- "Can I install a satellite dish?"
- "What's the pet policy?"
- "Can I paint my front door?"
- "Am I allowed to rent out my unit on Airbnb?"
- "What changes need board approval?"

**Schedules and dates**
- "When is trash pickup?"
- "When is the next board meeting?"
- "When was the roof last inspected?"

**Payments**
- "How much are my HOA dues?"
- "How do I set up autopay?"
- "When is the next payment due?"
- "How do I access the property tax payment portal?"

**Building and maintenance**
- "Who do I contact about a leak?"
- "How do I report a maintenance issue?"
- "What does my HOA fee cover?"

**Ownership (for TICs)**
- "What percentage of the building do I own?"
- "How are shared expenses divided?"

### Tips for Getting Good Answers

- **Be specific.** "Can I install a satellite dish on the roof?" works better than "What can I put on the roof?"
- **One question per email works best,** but asking multiple questions in one email is fine too.
- **Check the sources.** HouseKeep always tells you where it found the answer — which document and which section. You can verify for yourself.
- **If it doesn't know, it'll say so.** HouseKeep won't make things up. If the answer isn't in your HOA's records, it will tell you and suggest who to contact.

### What HouseKeep Cannot Do

- It cannot make changes, approve requests, or take actions (no deleting, no payments, no approvals)
- It cannot give legal advice (but it can show you exactly what the governing documents say)
- It does not respond when CC'd on an email thread unless you specifically ask it a question
- It cannot access your personal email — it only reads the HOA's shared inbox

You can do everything residents can do, plus:

### Ask About Finances
- "What's our current reserve fund balance?"
- "Are we on track with the annual budget?"
- "What did we pay for plumbing last time?"

- "Flag any expenses that look unusual compared to last year"

### Review Maintenance History
- "Show me all maintenance done on unit 3 in the last year"
- "When was the fire alarm system last inspected?"
- "List all outstanding maintenance requests"
- "What's the warranty status on the elevator repair?"

### Check Legal and Insurance
- "What did the attorney say about the fence dispute?"
- "What's our insurance policy number and when does it expire?"
- "Are there any outstanding violations?"

### Look Up Ownership Records
- "Who owns what percentage?"
- "Which units are owner-occupied vs. rented?"

### Using HouseKeep in Email Threads

You can CC HouseKeep on any email thread:

- **It will silently track and save the conversation.** It won't respond or chime in.
- **It won't interrupt.** HouseKeep only speaks when directly asked a question.
- **It remembers.** Later, you can ask "What was discussed in the thread about the roof repair?" and it will know.

This is a great way to automatically keep records of important discussions without any extra work.

### Forwarding Documents

When you receive an HOA document — an insurance renewal, an inspection report, a contractor bid — just forward it to HouseKeep.

1. Forward the email (with the attachment) to your HouseKeep address
2. HouseKeep automatically reads, categorizes, and files the document
3. You get a confirmation like: "Filed as Insurance Policy Renewal, expires December 2025"

Now anyone in the HOA can find that document just by asking.

## For the Secretary

### Meeting Notes

After a board meeting:

1. Forward your meeting notes to HouseKeep (email, Word doc, PDF, or even photos of handwritten notes)
2. HouseKeep will summarize them, extract action items, and file everything
3. Later, anyone can ask "What happened at the March board meeting?" and get a clear answer

### Preparing for Meetings

- Ask: "What action items came out of the last meeting?"
- Ask: "What decisions were tabled for follow-up?"
- Ask: "Draft an agenda for the next meeting based on open items"

### Automatic Record Keeping

- CC HouseKeep on important email threads to create a searchable record
- Forward important documents for automatic filing and categorization
- Ask: "What documents have been filed this month?" to review recent additions

## Using the Web Dashboard

For a richer experience beyond email, you can use the web dashboard.

### Signing In

1. Go to your HOA's dashboard URL (your admin will share this)
2. Click "Sign in with Google"
3. Use the same Google email address that's authorized for your HOA
4. You're in — no separate password to remember

### What You'll Find

**Chat**
A text box where you can type questions and get instant answers, just like email but in real time. Great for when you have several questions in a row.

**Your HOA's Documents**
Browse all documents organized by category:
- Your HOA Rules (CC&Rs, bylaws, house rules)
- Financial Records (budgets, assessments, invoices)
- Past Meetings (minutes, agendas, resolutions)
- Maintenance Records (work orders, inspections, photos)
- Insurance and Legal
- Vendor Contracts

Click any document to read it. Use the search bar to find specific topics across all documents.

**Maintenance Timeline**
A visual timeline of all maintenance work — what was done, when, with photos if available. Useful for seeing the full history of work on the building.

**Search**
Find anything across all documents and emails. Type a topic and see every mention across your HOA's entire history.

## For Administrators: Setting Up HouseKeep

### Initial Setup

1. **Connect your Gmail account** — Visit the setup page and sign into the HOA's Gmail account. Click "Allow" to give HouseKeep read access. This is a one-time action.
2. **Wait for ingestion** — HouseKeep reads all historical emails and attachments. For a medium-sized inbox (a few thousand emails), this takes 1-2 hours.
3. **Add authorized residents** — Enter each resident's name, email address, unit number, and role (resident, board member, or admin).
4. **Share the HouseKeep email address** — Send residents the welcome email so they know how to start using it.

### Managing Residents

- **New move-in:** Add their name, email, and unit to the authorized list
- **Move-out:** Remove or deactivate their email from the authorized list
- **Role change:** Update their role (e.g., when someone joins or leaves the board)

Access the admin panel via the web dashboard to manage residents.

### If HouseKeep Gets Something Wrong

- **Flag it:** In the audit log on the web dashboard, you can flag an incorrect answer
- **Correct it:** Provide the right answer, which helps improve future responses
- **Re-ingest:** If new historical documents are uploaded to Gmail, you can trigger a re-ingestion to update the knowledge base

### Reviewing Activity

The admin panel includes an audit log showing:
- Who asked what (and when)
- What HouseKeep answered
- Which documents were cited
- Any flagged or failed interactions

This helps you monitor quality and catch any issues early.

## Privacy and Security

**Your data is protected:**
- HouseKeep only responds to email addresses on the authorized list
- It verifies that emails are actually from who they claim to be (checking for spoofing)
- Sensitive financial and legal details are only shared with board members, not all residents
- Your data is encrypted and never shared with other HOAs
- HouseKeep never deletes, forwards, or modifies your emails
- Every interaction is logged so administrators can review what's happening

**What HouseKeep does not do:**
- It does not access anyone's personal email account
- It does not share your questions with other residents
- It does not send unsolicited emails
- It does not store passwords

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is this free?**
Yes. HouseKeep is free for all HOA residents and administrators.

**Do I need to download an app?**
No. Just send an email. You can also use the web dashboard in any browser if you prefer.

**How quickly does it respond to emails?**
Usually within 1-3 minutes. During initial setup (when it's processing historical emails for the first time), it may be slower.

**What if it gives a wrong answer?**
Every answer includes the source document and section, so you can verify. If something is wrong, let your HOA admin know — they can flag it and help improve future responses.

**Can it access my personal email?**
No. HouseKeep only reads the HOA's shared Gmail inbox. It has no access to any individual's personal email.

**Who can see my questions?**
Only HOA administrators can review the audit log, which shows who asked what. Your questions are not visible to other residents.

**What if someone who shouldn't have access emails it?**
HouseKeep checks every sender against the authorized list. If it doesn't recognize the email address, it responds with a generic "not authorized" message and does not share any HOA information.

**Can it send emails on behalf of the board?**
No. HouseKeep only responds to questions. It never initiates emails, forwards messages, or takes actions on its own.

**What if I'm not sure what to ask?**
Every response from HouseKeep includes 2-3 suggested follow-up questions. The web dashboard also shows sample questions organized by topic. Just start with whatever is on your mind — there's no wrong way to ask.

**My email doesn't seem to work with it.**
Make sure your HOA admin has added your email address to the authorized list. The email you send from must exactly match what's on file. If you're still having trouble, contact your HOA administrator.

**What kinds of files can it read?**
PDFs, Word documents, Excel spreadsheets, and images (JPG, PNG). It can read scanned documents and even photos of printed pages. It currently does not process video files.

**Is there a limit to how many questions I can ask?**
For practical purposes, no. There's a rate limit to prevent abuse (20 emails per hour), but normal usage will never hit it.
